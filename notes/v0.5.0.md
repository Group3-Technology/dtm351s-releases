## DTM 351 Teslameter Control 0.5.0

Desktop control and monitoring for the **Group3 DTM 351-S 3-axis digital
teslameter** over serial connections — three channels read together, a live
magnitude, real-time graphs, a 3D view of the field's direction, CSV logging,
and a configurable derived measure.

**First time here?** Download the file for your platform below, then follow
the [install steps](https://github.com/Group3-Technology/dtm351s-releases#installing)
— there's a one-time click to approve the app on first launch, because it
isn't code-signed. No installer and no admin rights are needed. The full
**User Guide** is included in the download, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` is attached
if you'd like to verify what you downloaded.

| Download | Platform |
|----------|----------|
| `G3_DTM351_Control-macos.zip` | macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer |
| `G3_DTM351_Control-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) |

The **Vector** window's 3D view of the field's direction needs OpenGL
graphics support. On a PC without it, the view says so and everything else
still works.
