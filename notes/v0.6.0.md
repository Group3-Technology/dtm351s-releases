## DTM 351 Teslameter Control 0.6.0

Adds a download for Intel Macs, a delayed start for Block captures, and
fixes that keep Block captures and the CSV log's timestamps on time.
Recommended for everyone who uses Block mode or logs to CSV.

| Download | Platform |
|----------|----------|
| `G3_DTM351_Control-macos.zip` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer, Apple Silicon (M1 or later) |
| `G3_DTM351_Control-macos-intel.zip` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer, Intel |
| `G3_DTM351_Control-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11 (64-bit) |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → **About This Mac** shows the chip
or processor and the macOS version. The
[install steps](https://github.com/Group3-Technology/dtm351s-releases#installing)
are unchanged.

### What's new

- **A download for Intel Macs.** `G3_DTM351_Control-macos-intel.zip` runs
  on Intel Macs with macOS 15 or newer.
- **Delayed start for Block captures.** In Block mode, tick **Delayed
  start** and set a date and time; START then arms the capture, and it
  begins on its own at that moment. While armed the status reads
  *Armed — starts at* that time and the acquisition settings are locked.
  STOP, changing mode, or any channel losing its connection cancels it. A
  time already past starts the capture at once. The time can be at most
  24.8 days ahead; a later one does not arm.

### Fixed

- **Block captures now keep to the chosen rate.** Rows could drift a
  reading early or late, and a 300-row capture at 1 Hz could finish more
  than a second late. Each row now lands on the rate's grid. Where a set of
  readings is incomplete the row is left out, as before, and the block
  still ends after the number of readings you asked for.
- **Fewer gaps at 10 Hz.** Once a second the app's own housekeeping could
  delay a reading by up to about 80 ms, which split or dropped a set of
  three readings. It no longer lands on the same tick as the temperature
  read.
- **`timestamp_iso` in the CSV log is now the time of the reading.** It
  was the time the row was written, which trailed the reading by a varying
  amount. It is now when the row's first reading was requested, in UTC from
  the PC clock. The column's format is unchanged. Over a long run it can
  differ from `t_seconds` by any correction made to the PC clock.
- **Connect all during an automatic reconnect** no longer leaves the old
  retry running in the background.

### Changed

- **Block rates in Hz are now 1, 2, 5 or 10, chosen from a list.**
  Readings arrive at 10 Hz and Block keeps every few, so other rates were
  not exact: 3 Hz actually ran at 3.33 Hz, and 4 or 6 Hz at 5 Hz. Rates in
  seconds are unchanged — any whole number up to 86 400 (one day).

### Upgrading

- **macOS needs version 15 (Sequoia) or newer.** The 0.5.0 notes said
  macOS 11; macOS 15 is the real minimum.
- **A saved Block rate that is no longer offered** (for example 3 Hz) is
  changed to the nearest rate offered, 2 Hz, the first time you open the
  app. Your other settings are kept.
